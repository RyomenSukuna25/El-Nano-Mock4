(01) Our story

An Ecuadorian home, since 2001.

Since 2001 we have cooked the coast of Ecuador with fresh seafood, plenty of lime, pickled onion and recipes passed from family to family.

Today we are seven houses across Spain and the United States — and we still serve encebollado the way we did on day one.
